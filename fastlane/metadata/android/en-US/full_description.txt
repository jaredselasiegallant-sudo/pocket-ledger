PocketLedger is a privacy-first, offline personal finance app built for Ghana and beyond.

Features:
- Automatic SMS transaction parsing for MTN MoMo, Vodafone Cash, AirtelTigo, and banks
- Real-time transaction capture from incoming SMS
- Multiple currency support (GHS, USD, EUR, GBP, NGN, KES, ZAR) with dynamic switching
- Interactive charts and spending analytics
- Category-based budget tracking
- CSV and PDF export
- Dark and light themes with Precision Utilitarian design
- No internet required — all data stays on your device
- Zero tracking, zero ads, zero data collection
- Open source under the MIT license

PocketLedger works completely offline. Your financial data never leaves your phone.
